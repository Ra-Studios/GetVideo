Your privacy.
In plain language.

Links and media

GetVideo uses the link you provide and your format, trim and subtitle choices to find and download the requested media. Media providers receive the requests needed to access their content. Using the browser version sends your link to the service that processes the request.

Files and history

Downloaded files and Vault details are kept on your device or in your browser storage. Sharing a file sends it to the app you choose. Files saved to your device’s Downloads folder remain there until you remove them with your file manager.

Permissions and clipboard

Notifications show download progress and completion. Older Android versions may require storage permission. Clipboard features help suggest or paste a copied link while the app is active. You can control app permissions in your device settings.

Updates and this website

The Android app checks GitHub for public updates. This website may contact GitHub to find the latest APK and opens GitHub for downloads. These services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Update checks do not send your media links, files or Vault history. This showcase website has no advertising or analytics scripts.

Support

Support links open the RA Studios support website. Information you provide there is handled by that website and any payment service you choose. For privacy questions, email contact@rastudios.site.
